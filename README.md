Objective

The objective of this lab is to understand how Generative AI can create concise summaries and management reports while maintaining complete fidelity to the original source.

The lab focuses on preserving important facts and figures, avoiding unsupported information, checking numerical accuracy, identifying omissions and inventions, using [VERIFY] placeholders, and creating structured management-report scaffolds.

Summary Fidelity

Summary fidelity means that an AI-generated summary accurately represents the original source without changing its meaning or adding unsupported information.

A faithful summary should:

Retain important facts and figures.
Avoid adding new facts.
Avoid changing the meaning of the source.
Include important limitations.
Avoid unsupported conclusions.
Avoid invented statistics or recommendations.
Clearly distinguish facts from interpretations.

A shorter summary is useful only when it remains faithful to the original source.

Source Passage — Airport Passenger-Service Report

During August, an airport customer-service team reviewed 120 passenger comments.

The comments included:

45 concerning long security-screening queues.
30 concerning unclear directional signs.
20 concerning baggage delays.
15 concerning limited seating near departure gates.
10 praising staff helpfulness.

The report did not measure overall passenger satisfaction, compare the airport with other airports, or calculate financial impact.

Airport management introduced two temporary information desks near the security area for a two-week trial. The effect of the trial had not been measured when the report was prepared.

Using [VERIFY] Placeholders

[VERIFY] placeholders were used whenever information was not available in the supplied source.

Examples:

[VERIFY WITH MANAGEMENT]
[VERIFY WITH FINANCE DEPARTMENT]
[VERIFY AFTER TRIAL EVALUATION]
[VERIFY — NOT MEASURED IN SOURCE]
[VERIFY WITH PREVIOUS REPORT]

These placeholders prevent unsupported information from being presented as a verified fact.

Key Learnings
Summary fidelity means accurately representing the original source.
Important facts and figures must be retained.
AI-generated numbers should be checked separately.
Omissions and inventions are different problems.
Source limitations should remain visible in summaries.
A concise summary can still be misleading if important limitations are removed.
Report scaffolds help organise information before completing a report.
Report structure should be separated from factual content.
[VERIFY] placeholders identify information that needs confirmation.
Recommendations should not be presented as verified facts unless authorised.
Human review is necessary before using AI-generated management reports.
Conclusion

This lab demonstrated how Generative AI can assist with professional aviation summaries and management-report preparation while maintaining source fidelity.

The activities showed the importance of preserving exact figures, identifying limitations, avoiding unsupported conclusions, separating report structure from facts, and using [VERIFY] placeholders for unavailable information.

AI-generated management reports should always receive human review to verify facts, figures, limitations and recommendations before professional use.
